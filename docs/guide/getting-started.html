Getting started

3 Steps Ahead is a free desktop app for Windows that reads the log MTG Arena writes on your PC. One file to install, one setting to turn on in Arena, and every game you play from then on is kept and analysed.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11, with MTG Arena installed on the same PC. Mac and Linux are not supported yet.
  • About 400 MB of free disk space: the app, the card data it downloads on first start, and your games as you play.
  • An internet connection on first start, and from time to time for card images, prices and updates.

Nothing else: the installer carries everything the app needs, Python included, and does not ask for administrator rights.

Install

  1. Download 3-Steps-Ahead-Setup.exe from the latest release.
  2. Run it. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway: the beta is not signed yet, so SmartScreen does not know it.
  3. Follow the setup. It installs for your Windows user only, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\3 Steps Ahead, with a shortcut in the Start menu and on the desktop.
  4. Start 3 Steps Ahead from the Start menu or the desktop.

Prefer the source? The same release has a zip of the code, with Install.bat to set it up on your own Python 3.11.

Turn on Detailed Logs in Arena

Arena writes the details of your games to its log only when this option is on. Without it the app sees that you logged in, but not your decks, hands and games.

  1. In MTG Arena, open Options (the gear at the top right).
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Tick Detailed Logs (Plugin Support).
  4. Restart Arena once, so the new log starts with the details.
Why this is allowed

It is the same option every Arena tracker asks for. The app only reads that file: it never reads Arena's memory, never changes its files, and never clicks or types in the game. Outside help is not allowed in sanctioned competitive events (Arena Championship and its qualifiers); there, close the app.

The first start

A small window prepares what the app needs, with a progress line for each step:

  • Arena's public card database, so every card id in your log has a name, a cost and a type.
  • Card data from Scryfall (about 125 MB): rules text, legality in every format, prices and the images you see when you hover a card.
  • The app's data pack: the Standard card pool and ban list, the set calendar. It is inside the zip and is refreshed with each update.

Then the app opens behind a short welcome window, four steps: Arena (it checks that Detailed Logs is on), Data sharing (share your games or keep them private: off unless you say yes), your collection (the Untapped.gg link, optional) and how to use the app. You can change all of it any time in the Settings tab. What is shared and what is never shared is written in Privacy.

The app opens on Your decks. It reads every Arena log it finds on your PC, so the games you played in the last days are there from the first minute.

How your games are kept

Arena overwrites its log each time it starts, and keeps only the previous one. While 3 Steps Ahead runs, it saves every finished game in its own folder, so nothing is lost.

  • Keep the app open while you play, or at least open it after a session. It notices a finished game within about 30 seconds and updates your decks and numbers.
  • If the app was closed, the games of your last two Arena sessions are still in Arena's two logs and are read the next time you open it.
  • If Arena was restarted more than twice while the app was closed, the games of the older sessions are gone from the logs, and the app cannot recover them.

Link your collection (optional)

Arena's log says when cards arrive from drafts and rewards, but it never lists your whole collection. To see which cards you own and which wildcards a deck still needs, link your public profile from Untapped.gg:

  1. On Untapped.gg, open your profile and copy its address. It looks like https://mtga.untapped.gg/profile/…/….
  2. In 3 Steps Ahead, paste it in the welcome window, or later in the Settings tab under Your collection.

The collection is downloaded at once and refreshed every 20 minutes, and the cards your log shows arriving afterwards are added on top. Your limited history comes from the same profile and appears after a restart of the app.

Without a link

Everything that comes from your games works. Where the app would need your collection (cards you own, wildcards a deck needs) it shows “-”.

If you have more than one Arena account

Each Arena account gets its own profile: games, decks, resources, wishlist and Deck builder are kept apart. The account menu in the top bar follows the account Arena is logged into. You can pick another account to look at it, or All accounts to see everything summed up. More in Social & accounts.

Updates

The app checks for a new version when it starts and every six hours. When there is one, the version button at the bottom right of every tab turns orange, Download version X, and opens the release page in your browser; when you have the latest it says Up to date. The app never downloads or installs anything by itself.

To update, close the app and run the new setup: it installs over the old version. Your games, decks, Boards and settings are in the Log_Arena folder of the app and stay as they are.

Uninstall

Close the app, then in Windows Settings › Apps › Installed apps choose 3 Steps Ahead and Uninstall. It removes the app, its private Python and the card data it downloaded. Your own data, the Log_Arena folder, stays in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\3 Steps Ahead, in case you reinstall: delete that folder too if you want everything gone.
