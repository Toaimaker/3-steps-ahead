Deck analysis

For every deck you play, the app goes through your own games card by card: which cards carry your wins, which ones stay stuck in hand, which show up in your losses, and which opposing cards and pairs beat you most. Every number comes from your log, nothing from other players.

Where to find it

Open a deck you have played (see the deck window) and unfold Analysis in the right column. Each category is a small fold of its own, closed at first, with the first card of its list written in the header so you can scan them without opening anything. The app remembers which folds you keep open.

The analysis is worked out in the background when the window opens; on a deck with a long history it takes a second or two.

The six categories

CategoryWhat it listsWhat to do with it
Best performersYour cards with the highest win rate in the games where you cast them, next to the win rate of the deck.These are the cards that make the deck work. Keep them, look for more of them.
Hard to castCards you saw in a game and never cast in that game: too expensive, wrong colours, or no right moment.A card that is often stuck may need a cheaper slot, more sources of its colour, or a different card.
Weak in your lossesYour cards that show up in lost games more than in won ones.Candidates to cut, or to bring in only against certain decks.
Their cards that beat youOpposing cards that appear in your losses more than expected. Cards that are in almost every game, and lands, are left out: they say nothing.What your sideboard should answer, or what to play around.
Unplayable · their combosTwo opposing cards that, seen together, lose you more games than each of them alone.The pairs to break: answer one piece before the other arrives.
Unplayable · your clunky pairsTwo of your cards that stay stuck in hand together.Usually two expensive or situational cards: one of them can go.

How it is counted

  • Which games: the games played with that deck, matched by the deck's id in Arena, not by its name. Renaming a deck does not split its history; two decks with the same name are not mixed.
  • Which cards: the cards seen in each game from the app's own record of that game. A card with two faces counts as one card.
  • “Seen” includes cards that went to the graveyard from the library, for example by surveil or mill: the game record does not separate them from the hand.
  • Win rates are compared with the deck's own win rate, so a card at 60% in a deck at 58% is not a star.
Few games, few certainties

Under 15 games the section says that its lists are hints. A card cast four times can easily be at 100% by chance. The lists become worth acting on after a few dozen games, and the opposing combos need even more.

An example

In one of our test decks, an Azorius deck with about 150 games in the log, Restoration Seminar wins 77% of the games where it is cast, well above the deck. But it stays in hand in 63% of the games where it shows up: it is the best card when it resolves and the hardest to cast. The answer is not to cut it but to make it castable more often: one land more, or a cheaper card in its place on the curve.

Tips

  • Read Best performers and Hard to cast together: the same card can be in both.
  • After a change to the deck, the old games still count. If you changed a lot, judge the new version on the games after the change, or make a new deck in Arena.
  • Hover any card name in the lists to see the card.
