Meta

The meta tab will show the most played decks of each format with their real win rates, built from the games that 3 Steps Ahead players choose to share. It is not filled yet.

Why it is empty for now

The public statistics of trackers and tournament sites belong to those sites, and their terms do not let another app redistribute them. So the app does not ship anyone else's meta. Your own games in every format are all there, in Your decks and Performance.

How it will fill

On first start the app asks whether you want to share game data (off by default, see Help and privacy). When enough players share, the meta is computed from those games: no names, no ids, and only from people who said yes.

Meanwhile: start from any list

Copy a deck list from Arena, MTGO or any site and press Import in Your decks: it goes to your wishlist with the wildcards it still needs, and Edit opens a copy in the Deck builder.

When the meta is there

Each meta deck will open in the same deck window as your own decks, with Edit, Add to wishlist, Export and Board.
