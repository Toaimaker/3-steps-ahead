Performance

Your history as a player: the win rate game after game with the leagues you reached, your numbers and prizes, and how each deck does in each event. Constructed and limited live in one tab; the Mode menu switches between them.

The menus at the top

MenuWhat it does
ModeStandard (your constructed games) or Limited. The tab opens on the mode of your last game.
SetAll sets, or one set period: everything below follows it. Sets are listed by release date, newest first.
Event (Standard)Ranked and Play by default; then every event you played (Ranked, Play, Dual Color Precons, Bot Match, Constructed Event…) and All events.
Which eventInside Constructed Event: the single events by their Arena name, to see which deck performs where.
Format (Limited)Quick Draft, Premier Draft, Traditional Draft, Sealed, Arena Cube and the other limited events you played, or all of them.

The win rate chart

Across, your games one after another; up, your win rate from the first game to that one, with the 50% line marked. In Standard the chart follows ranked play:

  • the league logos sit on the line at the game where you reached each league;
  • a demotion is marked under the line;
  • a new season is a dashed line with ↺ and the league you started from.

In Limited the chart covers all the games of the chosen set, with the dates of the drafts and a trophy at the end of each 7-win run. Move the pointer along the chart to read the game and the win rate at that point. With another event chosen, the chart follows that event, without leagues.

Your numbers

A grid of tiles, each with its name above the figure:

  • Win rate, games, wins, losses and the win/loss ratio;
  • Rank with the league logo (Standard) or trophies (Limited);
  • Gems won, packs won, rares and mythics from event rewards, read from Arena's log, with a note on the entry fees you paid.

Decks, meta and field

Next to the numbers, a list you choose with Show:

ShowWhat it lists
Your decksYour most played decks in the chosen event, best win rate first, each with a bar from 0 to 100% and a tick at 50%. In Limited, by colour pair. Click one to open its deck window.
MetaThe meta of the format chosen in the Format menu next to Show, as in the Meta tab.
FieldThe colours of the opponents you met in the chosen event, with their share and your record against them.
FarmingAppears once you have won prizes in constructed events: your decks with the events played, gems won minus gems spent, and packs.

In Limited the same place holds Meta and drafts, with three buttons: Meta (how the colour pairs of the set perform), Your drafts (each draft with its record) and Field.

Where the numbers come from

  • Constructed: your Arena log, game by game, from the day the app first read it. If you had linked an Untapped.gg profile, the games recorded there before your first log are added as history.
  • Limited: your linked Untapped.gg profile (drafts, records, trophies, field). Without a link, the limited view stays empty.
  • Prizes: the rewards Arena writes in its log.
A record is not a skill rating

Thirty games are a hint, a few hundred are a measure. The chart shows how your win rate settles as games pile up: watch the slope at the end, not the jumps at the start.
