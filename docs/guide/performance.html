Performance

An analyzer of your games and of the situations inside them, read from your Arena log. Build a situation with filters (on the draw, after a mulligan, attacking into open mana, with a removal in hand…) and every number, chart and table answers for those games only. Constructed and Limited sit side by side at the top.

The numbers on top

A row of tiles for the games you picked: win rate with its 95% interval, record and how far it is from all your games, game length, mulligan rate, win rate on the play and on the draw. Each name says what it measures (win rate, % of games, average turns). Under 30 games the row says a hint.

The ⚙ wheel on the right picks which numbers you see, all of them if you like, in one or two rows: among them win rate after a mulligan, keeping 7, fast wins, long games, concessions, lands kept, attack and defense gain, decided wins, how often you are on the play (tested against a coin flip), the longest losing streak, and lands drawn against chance.

Smart filters

On the left, grouped by the phases of a game: before the game (format, event, a win-rate window, By deck), opening and first turns (start, mulligans, lands in hand, result), the opponent and the fight (archetype, colours), the end of the game (length, rank), when (date, time of day, game in the session). A menu with a value chosen turns blue.

  • + more on each phase opens its extra filters: cheap spells and creatures in hand, land drops missed, curve, who attacked first, instants on the other player's turn, attacks into open mana, blocks, how the game ended, the day, the break before the game, the game before (a win, a loss, the first of the session) and the losses in a row before it.
  • Win rate: type a percentage, with ≤ or ≥, and the analyzer keeps the most recent run of games that makes that win rate. Then every other filter works inside it.
  • By deck opens a window with your decks as tiles (by use, win rate or name) and, on the right, vs all or vs colors: tick the colours and you get one exact variant of their deck, from mono-colour to five colours, each with its record on the play and on the draw.
  • Advanced filters opens a window with two tabs, You and Opponent, each split in phases: the opening hand (creatures, instants, sorceries, artifacts, enchantments, planeswalkers, removal), what each player cast on their turns and on the other's turns 1 to 5 (build lines with + and Add line), draws, attacks with the untapped lands of the defender, blocks, activated abilities and when, and the game: land drops, life, creatures at your turns 3/5/7, removal, board wipes, counters, cards left in hand, the leaks, graveyard and exile. On top the question you are asking, written out, with its answer.
  • Save as my filter keeps the situation; Clear all filters and Reset all go back to every game.

The folders

FolderWhat it shows
ChartWin rate, games, average turns or mulligan rate, game by game or by date, cumulative or over the last 10/20/50 games; Split by a variable draws one line per value. League logos and draft trophies on the line (switches Ranks, Trophies), vs filter to compare with all games, Gained by decisions. Under the chart the games of the situation: date, deck, opponent's colours, start, mulligan, result, turns, the opening hand and the board at the end. Click a game to open the replayer.
SpotsPreset searches in groups (opening hand, attack, defense, instant speed, life, board and removal), each with the win rate it would give now: one click sets its filters and opens the view that answers. Auto spots: where you win and lose the most, single conditions and pairs, kept only if they hold against the rest of your games.
ReportYour games grouped by any variable (deck, opponent, mulligan, card in hand, deck version…), sortable; click a row to filter it, double-click to watch its games in the replayer.
MatchupsYour decks against the archetypes you met (or only the meta ones): win rate cells, grey under 5 games. Click a cell to filter, double-click to watch.
CompareA (your filters) against B: everything else, all your games, a saved filter or, in Limited, 17Lands players on the same sets and colours (17Lands public data, CC BY 4.0). Every measure with the difference and whether it holds, or how many games would tell.
CardsRows of real cards: most found in hand, your turns 1 to 4, most cast (with the average turn of the first cast and the turns it waited in hand), cast by turn 4, finishers, the opponent's most played cards, harder to cast, drawn vs not drawn (your personal IWD), what your removal took, and the threats that beat you with Answers ›: the removal you own that answers them. Pairs › on a row shows the pairs and trios that come together.
DecisionsYour attacks and blocks by situation, mulligans, Costliest (the decisions that cost you most chance to win, with What would you do? to replay them as a quiz), Leaks (how often each leak happens, your win rate with and without it, the trend and how to fix it, Watch › to see them) and Graveyard (a reanimation ready, cards playable from exile, graveyards exiled).
LuckLands drawn by your turns 3, 5 and 7 against what chance would give, and your opening hands against a random deal and Arena's Bo1 smoothing.
My filtersYour saved filters with their numbers now, and the spots you bookmarked in the replayer.

Every folder can be detached into its own window (the mark on its tab) and follows the filters from there.

Leaks and the cost of a decision

The leaks are the mistakes coaches look for in replays, found in your log: a land drop missed with a land in hand, mana left unused with a castable creature, a creature cast before attacking, a chump block at high life, an unfavourable trade, an instant at the wrong time, a concession with outs, lands tapped the wrong way. They are clues, not verdicts: each one shows the number next to it.

The cost of a decision is how much your chance to win moved from that attack, block or spell to the next moment, against your average for the same kind of decision at the same stage of the game. The chance to win comes from a model trained on 17Lands public replay data (CC BY 4.0) from limited sets: in constructed it compares two boards, it is not a promise.

Where the numbers come from

  • Your Arena log, game by game, from the day the app first read it: hands, draws, every spell, attack and block. Limited and constructed alike.
  • Untapped.gg, if you linked your profile: the games recorded there before your first log are added as history.
  • 17Lands public data (CC BY 4.0, 17lands.com) for the Limited comparison and the win-probability model. 17Lands does not endorse this app.
A record is not a skill rating

Thirty games are a hint, a few hundred are a measure. Grey numbers mean too few games; «holds» means the difference is unlikely to be chance. A filter shows what happened in those games, not why.
