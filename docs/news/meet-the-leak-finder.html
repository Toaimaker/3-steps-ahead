Meet the Leak Finder

The Performance tab has a new name and a sharper job. The Leak Finder reads your Arena log, finds the mistakes that cost you games, tells you how often you make each one and what it costs, and opens the replay on the spot.

Leaks, ranked by what they cost

A missed land drop with a land in hand, mana left unused with a castable creature, a chump block at high life, an unfavourable trade, an instant at the wrong time, a concession with outs. Each leak gets a card that says it the way you would: what happened in your games, how often, your win rate with it and without it, and whether you are making it less lately. Below: what it is, why it costs, an example and how to fix it.

The list is sorted by how many points of your win rate the leak takes away, so Limited and Constructed each rank leaks with their own numbers.

Clues, not verdicts

A play that looks like a mistake can be a choice. Before calling it a leak, the Leak Finder looks ahead to the opponent's next turns: mana held up for an instant that was then cast, a blocker kept home that then blocked, a land kept for a card that needed it. Synergies count too: a creature cast before combat to pump the attackers is not a leak.

You against other players

In Limited each leak shows how often 17Lands players make it, from their public replay data (CC BY 4.0). Constructed has no public replays to compare with yet, and the card says so.

Not while you play

The Leak Finder is for reviewing your games. While MTG Arena or MTGO is open, the tab is covered and its windows hide; they come back when the game closes.
