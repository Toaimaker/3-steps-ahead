Shared games are live

Players who choose to share their games now send them to the 3 Steps Ahead server. The goal: a Meta tab built from real games played with the app, instead of copies of other sites' data.

Off until you say yes

Sharing is off by default. Turn it on or off any time with the switch in Settings › Data sharing. If it stays off, nothing leaves your PC.

What is sent

  • the event, its format (Standard, Historic, limited…), whether it is best-of-three, and the day, without the time;
  • your Arena rank when the game started, such as Gold 3;
  • the result, who started, mulligans and turns;
  • your deck list, your kept opening hand and the opposing cards you saw.

It never sends your in-game name, your Arena account id or your opponents' names. Your account is replaced by a one-way code, the same for all your games: the data is pseudonymous, not anonymous. Before leaving, games wait in a queue you can read on your PC.

Where it goes

On Cloudflare, in a database restricted to the European Union. Numbers from shared games will be published only for groups of players, once enough different players share; no single player's games are shown on their own.

Your rights

You can ask to see or delete your games at any time, quoting the code shown in Settings. The privacy notice has every detail.

Since version 0.1.91

Shared games carry the format, best-of-three and rank, so the numbers can be split format by format and league by league. Games shared before that were sent again once, complete.
