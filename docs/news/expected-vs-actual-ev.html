Leak Finder: expected against actual EV

The Leak Finder no longer hands out verdicts. It reads every game as a count of win-probability points, from the hand you kept to the last turn, and shows where you gained more than the same spot usually gives and where you left value on the table.

From the game down to the card

Every game starts from the win chance of your opening hand and ends at 100 or 0. Every turn, yours and the opponent's, moves that chance, and so does every phase inside it: main phase 1, combat, main phase 2. The points add up exactly from the game to the turns to the phases, so nothing is counted twice. The expected value is what the same turn or phase usually gives in your games of that type, from the same situation, from far behind to far ahead.

On the chart

A new metric, EV vs expected, sits next to Win rate and Games. Cumulative, a line that climbs means those games gain more than usual; one that falls means they lose value. EV of picks the turns or the phase, for example only your attacks or only your defense, and Split by draws one line per deck or per opponent.

What each card is worth

In Cards, every card has its EV while it was on the battlefield, how often it survived, and its context: cast with a partner in play or alone, cast at once or after waiting in hand. Merits › shows what each deed of a card is worth, measured on your games and not written by hand: winning a combat, trading, hitting unblocked, the lethal, a block that saved you, drawing a removal spell, dying for nothing. Each deed is the board after it against the same board without it.

The opponent's cards get the same reading, and Lists › rebuilds their decks from the cards you saw, each card with how often it showed up.

From a spot to what came next

Build a spot in Advanced filters, now with the phase of the turn, and under the answer you see what happened next: how often your creatures survived the opponent's next turn, which removal spells took them, and the EV of those turns. The table under the chart shows the EV of your spot in every game, green when it gained and red when it lost, with the card that made the most and the least EV.

The phase, step by step

Since 0.1.126 the phase of a «cast» line in Advanced filters goes down to the step: upkeep, draw step, main phase 1, beginning of combat, declare attackers, declare blockers, combat damage, end of combat, main phase 2, end step, or combat as a whole.

Is this spot worth it?

A new number on top of the page, EV value (pick it with ⚙), turns the EV of the games you filtered into a percentage: how much win chance that spot adds or takes away per game, against the usual. Next to it a verdict: worth it, avoid, or too close to call when the games are not enough to tell.

In the replayer

The diamonds on the timeline are now the phases that gained or lost the most EV against the usual. Leaks stay in the story of each step, as an explanation, not as a verdict.

A difference from the expected value is not a mistake: a good choice can meet a bad outcome. The win chance comes from a model trained on public 17Lands replays (CC BY 4.0) of Limited games; in Constructed it compares moments with each other.
