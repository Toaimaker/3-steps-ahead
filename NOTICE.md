## Data sources

- Card data and images: [Scryfall](https://scryfall.com), downloaded by each user's PC. Not affiliated with Scryfall. Deck covers show the artist under the art.
- Arena card database: read from the MTG Arena client installed on your PC (its local card database file, read only).
- Untapped.gg: only if you link your own public profile, your collection and games are read from it for you. No Untapped.gg data is included in this package. Not affiliated.
- No metagame data or draft ratings from third-party sites are included in this package.
